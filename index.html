The programme

Inside thesix weeks.

The plan is not handed over and forgotten. Here is the rhythm you can expect.

Week 01

Plan goes live

Your routine and nutrition land, and you're walked through both. Nothing is left for you to interpret on your own.

Week 02

First form review

You send videos of your key lifts and get them corrected — before poor habits become permanent ones.

Week 03

First adjustment

Loads, volume and intake are re-tuned against what actually happened in the first fortnight, not what was predicted.

Week 04

Halfway review

An honest look at the numbers and the mirror. What's working gets doubled down on, what isn't gets replaced.

Week 05

Intensity earned

Where your technique and recovery allow it, the work gets harder. This is the week the progress starts to show.

Week 06

Results and what's next

Where you started, where you finished, and a clear plan for holding it — with or without continued coaching.
